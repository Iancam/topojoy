# TopoJoy
Join a generosity experiment! If you have a friend who enjoys wilderness, or a place in the woods that matters to someone you know, give them a gift, a map, to commemorate the spot.

# How it works
You select the location and trails, the hand off to me. I create a series of map options for your review. You select your favorite, and then you select print options, input an address, and I'll print it and mail it to the recipient. # Pricing
This is an economy of gifts. The ability to make a map is a gift, and the map itself is a gift you give. If, later, you feel like paying it forward, you can donate here which helps keep the experiment alive, and lets me know that you appreciate the gift. But if you want to extend the experiment further, you can pay it forward in another way, by giving somewhere else in your life.

# Why
Generosity is a practice that brings me and many others a lot of joy. I want to share that with you.
