# TopoJoy

Join a generosity experiment! If you have a friend with a special place in the wilderness, give them a map to commemorate the spot.

# What it costs

This is an economy of gifts; I'm not charging anything. The ability to make a map is a gift from a donor, and the map itself is a gift you give. Donors benefit from giving, so there are no strings attached. If you recognize a debt of gratitude, or a responsibility/desire to keep this goodness alive, you can pay it forward to others, or you can [become a donor](/donate) too. Because TopoJoy runs on donations, map prints are limited.

# How it works

You select the location and trails. Optionally, add some background text, like poetry or a personal message. I'll make several designs for you to choose from, then I'll print and mail your favorite. Afterwards, I'll ask how the gift affected you and the recipient.

# Why

TopoJoy began as a gift: a topographic map of my sister's favorite trail, in her favorite colors, with songs she used to sing. Everything since has been an experiment in generosity. [Read why →](/origin-story)
