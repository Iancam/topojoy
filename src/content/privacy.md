# Privacy Policy

_Last updated: February 4, 2026_

This Privacy Policy explains how Topojoy ("we", "us") collects, uses, and shares information when you use our website and services (the "Service").

## 1. Information we collect

### Account and profile information

- Contact details such as your name and email address when you sign in or create an account via our authentication provider.

### Order and map information

- Map designs you create, including configuration settings, titles, and labels.
- Order details such as products, quantities, pricing, shipping address, and order status.

### Usage information

- Log data about how you use the Service (for example, pages viewed, actions taken, timestamps) and basic device/browser information.

### Payment information

Payments are processed by our third-party payment provider. We do not store full payment card numbers on our servers. Limited transaction identifiers and metadata may be stored to reconcile orders and handle support.

## 2. How we use information

We use information to:

- Provide, operate, and improve the Service.
- Create and fulfill your print orders.
- Communicate with you, for example about orders, updates, and support requests.
- Maintain the security and integrity of the Service, including detecting and preventing fraud or abuse.

## 3. How we share information

- **Print and shipping partners.** We share the minimum necessary order and shipping details with our print and logistics partners so they can manufacture and deliver your prints.
- **Service providers.** We use third-party providers for hosting, analytics, authentication, and payment processing. These providers may process your information on our behalf under appropriate safeguards.
- **Legal and safety.** We may disclose information if we believe it is reasonably necessary to comply with law, regulation, legal process, or governmental request; to protect the rights, property, or safety of us, our users, or others; or to enforce our Terms of Service.

## 4. Cookies and similar technologies

We and our providers may use cookies or similar technologies to keep you signed in, remember preferences, and understand how the Service is used. You can typically control cookies through your browser settings, but disabling certain cookies may affect functionality.

## 5. Data retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Service, fulfill orders, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. We may retain aggregated or anonymized information for analytics and service improvement.

## 6. Your choices

- You can update certain account details and map configurations within the app.
- You may request deletion of your account and associated data, subject to legal and contractual requirements.

## 7. Children’s privacy

The Service is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

## 8. International transfers

Your information may be processed and stored on servers located in countries other than where you live. We take reasonable steps to ensure appropriate protections are in place for such transfers.

## 9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make material changes, we will update the date at the top of this page and may provide additional notice within the Service. Your continued use of the Service after changes take effect means you accept the updated policy.
